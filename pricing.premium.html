Pricing FAQs

Can you customize pricing?

Absolutely. These are starting points. We customize engagement scope, timeline, and fees to match your specific needs and budget constraints.

What if we need ongoing support?

We offer retainer and advisory models for ongoing partnership. Many clients transition to annual retainers for continuous strategic guidance.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We can structure payments across engagement milestones or monthly installments depending on the scope and duration.

What about scope creep?

We establish clear scope statements upfront with change management processes. Any scope changes are discussed and adjusted collaboratively.

What's your ROI guarantee?

While we don't offer guarantees (outcomes depend on execution), our fees are structured to align with measurable value creation for clients.

How do you bill for travel?

Travel costs are either included in the fixed fee or billed separately depending on engagement scope and location.
